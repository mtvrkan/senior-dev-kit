# /deep-research

Research this topic thoroughly: $ARGUMENTS

Process:

1. State the research question clearly.
2. Fan out across multiple search angles and sources.
3. Read primary sources — not just summaries.
4. Cross-verify key claims across 2+ independent sources.
5. Synthesize a cited report.

Output:
RESEARCH QUESTION: [one sentence]
SOURCES CONSULTED: [numbered list with URLs]
KEY FINDINGS: [with citations]
CONTRADICTIONS / GAPS: [if any]
SUMMARY: [3-5 sentences]
CONFIDENCE: high | medium | low [reason]

Do not fabricate sources or data.
Mark unverified claims as [UNVERIFIED].
