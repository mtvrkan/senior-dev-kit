# /article-write

Write an article or long-form content on: $ARGUMENTS

Process:

1. Produce an outline first — do not skip.
2. Verify key facts before writing.
3. Write section by section in the requested tone.
4. End with word count and any facts that need review.

Output format:

```text
TITLE: [proposed]
AUDIENCE: [who this is for]
TONE: [technical | casual | formal | persuasive]
OUTLINE: [sections]
---

[full content]
---

WORD COUNT: [approximate]
REVIEW NOTES: [facts to verify]
```

Rules:

- Do not fabricate facts, statistics, or quotes.
- If writing in Turkish: natural language, not machine-translated tone.
- Active voice preferred.
